## Company Overview
- BookLeaf Publishing is a self-publishing company operating in India and the US.
- Publishing packages: Standard Free (no upfront cost) and Bestseller Breakthrough (premium, paid package with marketing and distribution add-ons).
- BookLeaf handles cover design, typesetting, ISBN assignment, printing, distribution, and royalty management.
- In-house printing facility and warehouse are in Delhi. Print partners: Repro India and Epitome Books.

## Royalty Policy
- 80/20 royalty split: 80% of net profit per book goes to the author, 20% to BookLeaf.
- Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon/Flipkart), and shipping charges.
- Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of the quarter ending.
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹1,000. Below this, royalties roll over to the next quarter.
- Payouts are made by bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard.
- Authors can view a detailed royalty breakdown in their dashboard, with sales figures per platform.

## ISBN Policy
- Every book published through BookLeaf receives a unique ISBN assigned by BookLeaf.
- ISBNs are registered under BookLeaf's publisher imprint. An author who wants an ISBN under their own imprint must obtain it independently.
- An ISBN error (duplicate, wrong book linked) is a high-priority issue and is escalated to the production team.

## Printing & Quality
- In-house printing handles most orders. Overflow or specific formats go to Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround: 5-7 business days from order confirmation.
- For quality issues (misprints, binding defects, color inconsistency), BookLeaf arranges a free reprint after verification. The author may need to share photos of the defective copy.

## Distribution & Availability
- Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings typically go live within 7-10 business days after publication is complete.
- A book showing as unavailable on a platform usually indicates a stock sync issue. BookLeaf can trigger a re-sync within 24-48 hours.

## Production Stages
- Stages: Manuscript Received → Editing (if opted) → Cover Design → Typesetting → Proofreading → ISBN Assignment → Printing → Distribution Setup → Published & Live.
- Authors are updated at each stage via email.
- Delays typically happen at Cover Design (waiting for author approval) and Proofreading (revision rounds).

## Communication Tone Guidelines
- Always empathetic and professional. Authors are partners, not customers to be managed.
- Acknowledge the author's concern before jumping to solutions.
- Be specific: include actual numbers, dates, and statuses rather than vague reassurances.
- If something is BookLeaf's fault (delayed royalties, ISBN error), own it directly. No corporate deflection.
- If the issue needs escalation or investigation, give a clear timeline ("Our team will look into this and get back to you within 48 hours"), not open-ended promises.
- Always end with a clear next step for the author and/or the BookLeaf team.

## Sample Examples
### Royalty & Payments
Query: "I published my book 4 months ago and still haven't received any royalty."
Response: Acknowledge the frustration. Explain the quarterly cycle and 45-day payout window. Check if bank details are linked. Give the specific next payout date. If genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
Query: "My royalty seems too low. I sold 200 copies but only received ₹3,000."
Response: Explain net profit (MRP minus printing, platform commission, shipping). Offer a detailed line-by-line breakdown. Don't be defensive; offer transparency.

### ISBN & Metadata Issues
Query: "My book shows a different ISBN on Amazon than the physical copy."
Response: Treat as high priority. Acknowledge this is a serious data issue. Confirm escalation to the production team immediately. Give a 48-hour resolution timeline.

### Printing & Quality
Query: "My author copies arrived with blurry images and misaligned pages."
Response: Apologise sincerely. Ask for photos of the defective copies. Confirm a free reprint once verified. Give a 5-7 business day timeline for the reprint.

### Distribution & Availability
Query: "My book is published but shows 'Currently Unavailable' on Amazon."
Response: Explain this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm you are triggering a re-sync with the distribution team. Set expectation: 24-48 hours to go live again.

### Book Status & Production Updates
Query: "It's been 3 weeks and my book is still in typesetting."
Response: Check the actual production status. If delayed, be honest about why (e.g., waiting on author approval for proofs). Give a specific updated timeline. Don't blame the author; frame it collaboratively.

### General Inquiry
Query: "Can I update the description of my book on Amazon after it's live?"
Response: Yes, metadata updates can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. Changes typically reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.
